requestAccountDeletion

requestAccountDeletion(input: RequestAccountDeletionInput!): AccountDeletionSchedule!

Schedule the signed-in account for deletion once the grace period ends (14 days unless the operator set RIM_ACCOUNT_DELETION_GRACE_DAYS, 0 to 30). Every other session of the account ends now; this one keeps working, and cancelAccountDeletion keeps the account until the grace period ends. me.deletionScheduledFor shows the pending deletion.

Then the account is purged: its sessions, devices, keys, workspace memberships, plaintext messages, files and personal settings are removed, workspaces where it was the only member are deleted, and its username becomes free. The user row stays as a tombstone (deleted-<id prefix>, "Deleted account", no email, password or key) that cannot sign in and shows in no member list or search; it stays the author of the account's encrypted messages, which are kept so the conversations' history does not shift, and of a deleted message others replied to. The tombstone keeps the account's key identity id, so the same key identity cannot sign up on this server again.

Errors

  • UNAUTHENTICATED: no session.
  • FORBIDDEN: a person from another server, who deletes their account at their relay.
  • VALIDATION: confirmUsername is not the account's username, or the account owns a workspace that has other members: hand it over (transferWorkspaceOwnership) or delete it (deleteWorkspace) first.
  • CONFLICT: a deletion is already pending.
